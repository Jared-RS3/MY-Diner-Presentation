POSITIONING

We are making the existing
journey work harder.

Nothing that currently works for MYDiners gets thrown away. The website sits alongside it and removes the friction between interest and a booked table.

We already have a PDF menu. KEEP IT

We add a faster, searchable mobile menu that makes it easier for customers to browse food and move straight into booking. The PDF stays available for download.

We already take bookings over the phone. KEEP PHONE BOOKINGS

We add 24/7 online booking for customers who want to book immediately, or when the restaurant is busy, full or closed.

We already have Instagram. EXCELLENT

Instagram creates attention. The website gives that attention somewhere designed to convert it into a reservation.

We already have a POS. KEEP IT

We are not replacing the restaurant's point of sale. Nothing about the way MYDiners takes payment changes.

The journey does not end
at the confirmed booking.

A confirmed table is the middle of the journey, not the end of it. The same system that took the booking can carry the customer through the visit and back again.

Seeing you tonight at 19:00. Need to change anything? Reply or call the restaurant.
