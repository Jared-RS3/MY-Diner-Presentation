    Some clauses need your particular attention.

    Clauses 5, 7, 12, 14 and 15 are marked ◆. They limit liability, place a risk on the Client, contain an indemnity, or record something the Client acknowledges. Section 49 of the Consumer Protection Act requires terms like these to be brought to your attention before you sign. Please read them, ask about anything that is unclear, and initial each one.

    This summary makes the agreement easy to understand. It does not replace the numbered clauses, which are the binding terms.

    The deposit Clause 5

    The commencement payment starts the project. Once work begins it is non-refundable, except in a legal cooling-off period, if Daybreak cancels, or where this agreement or the law requires a refund.

    The final payment Clause 4

    The balance is due when MYDiners gives final approval, before the website goes live.

    A fixed fee Clause 3

    The fee covers the agreed deliverables. If in-scope work takes longer than estimated, that is Daybreak's risk, not an extra invoice.

    No surprise costs Clause 3

    Nothing outside the agreed scope is charged unless MYDiners has approved the cost and timeline in writing first.

    Cancelling Clause 5

    MYDiners may cancel at any time in writing. The deposit is kept, and work done beyond its value is paid for. Nothing more.

    You own the work Clause 9

    When the project fee is paid in full, ownership of the custom website work transfers to MYDiners.

    Your accounts stay yours Clause 7

    Domain, Google, Dineplan and similar accounts are held in MYDiners' name, with Daybreak given access to do the work.

    Customer data Clause 11

    MYDiners controls its customers' personal information. Daybreak handles it only on MYDiners' instructions, as an operator under POPIA.

    No promised outcomes Clause 12

    Daybreak promises the work, done properly. It cannot promise rankings, bookings, reviews or revenue.

    Liability is capped Clause 14

    Each side's liability is capped at the fees paid in the previous 12 months, except for gross negligence, fraud and anything the law does not allow to be limited.

    If something is wrong Clauses 8 · 20

    Defects reported within 30 days of launch are fixed at no charge. Disputes start with a meeting, and your consumer-law rights stay intact.

    The retainer is optional Clause 16

    Month to month, starts after launch, and either side can end it on 30 days' written notice.
